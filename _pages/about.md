I am a Postdoctoral Researcher at the University of Ottawa working in fluid mechanics, computational fluid dynamics (CFD), and data-driven modeling for environmental and coastal applications.

My research focuses on turbulent buoyant jets and dense jet discharges, including desalination outfalls, mixing behavior, and near-field/far-field interaction under ambient crossflow.

On this website you can find:

- Publications and ongoing research outputs
- Talks and teaching activities
- An interactive dense-jet simulation tool

You can open the simulator directly here: [Dense Jet Simulator](/dense-jet-simulator/).

I welcome research collaborations on dense discharges, environmental hydraulics, and physics-informed modeling.
