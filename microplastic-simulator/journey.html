Teaching demonstration river to lake

Microplastic journey

A schematic, side-view visualization of how microplastic particles travel through a river and into a lake. Particles are advected by the flow, mixed by turbulence, and trapped by aquatic vegetation. Depending on their polymer density, size, and biofouling, they float at the surface, stay suspended, or settle to the bed, where river shear can resuspend them. A live fate budget tracks where every released particle ends up.

Illustrative demonstration only. This animation is a simplified, conceptual visualization intended for teaching and outreach. It is not based on real scientific data, has not been validated against experiments or field observations, and must not be used for research, design, or regulatory purposes. For the research model, see GPU microplastic transport.
